Keep the edit clear

Documentary text animation works best when the viewer can follow the subject. MATCH CUT keeps the target in one fixed screen-space box and avoids camera shake or animated zoom. The surrounding article changes on a regular timeline, with a subtle blur toward the outer crop.

A frame-synchronized sound event accompanies each page change. Pick a restrained documentary bed, a paper texture, or a quieter preset depending on the tone of the story.

Check the sources before publishing

The tool searches publicly accessible article pages and reconstructs short excerpts for the visual sequence. Source links are shown with the result. If source search cannot find enough matches, fictional editorial layouts may fill the sequence and are labeled as generated content.

That distinction matters in documentary work: the video is a designed text animation, not evidence that an article or publication reported a particular claim. Review the linked material and add any context your audience needs.
