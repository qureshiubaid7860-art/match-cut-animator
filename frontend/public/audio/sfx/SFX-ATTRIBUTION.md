# Sound-effect attribution

The three typewriter effects below use edited excerpts of recordings marked
CC0 1.0 on Freesound (public domain dedication):

- **Typewriter keys** — “Typewriter - single key - type 2.wav” by yottasounds,
  Freesound sound 380137:
  https://freesound.org/people/yottasounds/sounds/380137
- **Typewriter carriage return** — “Typewriter bell & carriage reset” by
  knufds, Freesound sound 345955:
  https://freesound.org/people/knufds/sounds/345955
- **Typewriter key + bell** — “typewriter bell” by MasterNavigator, Freesound
  sound 444813:
  https://freesound.org/people/MasterNavigator/sounds/444813

All are CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The clips in this folder are mono 48 kHz WAVs. The three recordings above were
trimmed, resampled, normalized, and layered into short, frame-synchronized
effects. Other effects are original project-generated audio.

**Mouse Click** — supplied by the project owner as
`soundreality-sound-of-mouse-click-4-478760.mp3`; converted to mono 48 kHz WAV
and trimmed to the click and its short tail for frame-synchronized playback.
The original source and license were not independently verified.
