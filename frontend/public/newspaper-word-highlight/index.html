Why highlight the same word?

A repeated term gives viewers a simple thread to follow through a fast sequence. Each page supplies new editorial context; the shared word connects those pages. The marker makes the repeated target easy to spot without adding an interface box around the text.

MATCH CUT finds the word position with OCR, draws an irregular highlighter stroke behind it, then aligns the article image to a consistent center point. Text outside the focus area gets a light softening effect, while the phrase remains the sharp focal point.

Choose a phrase that reads well

A specific phrase can make the visual sequence feel more connected than a broad, common word. Keep it short enough to read quickly and to fit comfortably inside the frame. The generator accepts up to four words.

Generated editorial pages are identified in the result notes. Public source reconstructions link to their original pages, so you can inspect the excerpt behind each frame.
